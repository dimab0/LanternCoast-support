STRATEGY · IPHONE AND IPAD

Run the lights of a winter coast.

Midwinter, and the sun won't rise for weeks. Put up stations on headlands and offshore rocks, keep the shipping lanes lit through reef and pack ice, and make the oil money last.

1

Build

Lamp, sweep or sector station. Each has a price, a reach and an oil bill.

2

Budget

Ships pay on arrival, oil drains every second. Run dry and every light drops to 60%.

3

Weather

Fog and snow squalls shrink every reach, on a schedule you can see coming.

Twelve coasts, one fair clock

Each coast is its own map, from a single ferry run to six-harbour straits. The rules run on a fixed simulated clock, so the same decisions score the same at any speed. Every coast has a Game Center ranking, and one more for the total.

Price

Coasts 1 to 3 are free. The full-coast pass, one in-app purchase, opens coasts 4 to 12.
