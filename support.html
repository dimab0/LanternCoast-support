How do I build a light?

Tap one of the glowing circles on a headland or an offshore rock, then choose a light. Its golden pool on the water shows exactly how far it reaches. You can build while the game is paused.

Why did a ship run aground?

Red dashed stretches of a lane, marked with a ◆, are reef or shoal in the dark. Ships crossing them can ground. Light them and they turn green. Fog shrinks every light's reach, and a network out of oil burns at 60%.

How do I aim a sector light?

Tap the light, then drag the round handle at the end of its beam, or use the turn buttons in its panel.

How is the score made?

Ships delivered × 100, cargo × 10, the average share of lit hazard × 5, credits left ÷ 10, less 150 for each grounding. Bring the goal number of ships home to open the next coast.

I bought the full coast but the coasts are locked.

Open Settings on the first screen, or the unlock screen, and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Each coast also opens only once the one before it is won.

My score is not on the leaderboard.

Leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device, then finish a coast. Your best scores are kept on the device either way.
