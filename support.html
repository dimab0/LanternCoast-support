Questions and answers

Still stuck? Email braznichenko@gmail.com.

How do I build a station?

Tap an open site (a ring on a headland or an offshore rock) and choose Cape Lamp, Sweep Light or Sector Light. Its pool of light on the water is exactly how far it reaches.

How do I aim a sector station?

Select it and drag the round handle at the end of its beam, or use the turn buttons on its card. It turns in 5° steps.

Why did a ship run aground?

It crossed reef or shoal (red dashes with a ◆) that no light covered. Bring those stretches inside a lit pool and they turn green. Fog and squalls shrink every pool, so check the forecast chip at the top.

Why did all my lights dim?

Credits ran out and oil couldn't be paid, so every station dropped to 60% reach. Lower some power settings or pull down a station (half its price comes back).

How is a shift scored?

100 per ship delivered, 10 per unit of cargo, up to 500 for how much of the lanes stayed lit, a tenth of the credits left, minus 150 for every grounding. Stars for the goal, 80% of par and par.

I bought the full-coast pass but coasts are locked.

Open Station settings (gear icon on the title screen) or the pass page and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

My score isn't in Rankings.

Rankings use Game Center: sign in under Settings › Game Center. Scores made while signed out stay on the device and are posted when you next play signed in.

The flashing bothers me.

Turn on Calmer lights in Station settings: no sweeping beams, flicker or lightning. The rules are unchanged.
