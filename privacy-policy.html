Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The short version

Lantern Coast does not collect any data. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored on your device

Your best score and stars on each coast, and your settings (sound, haptics, reduced flashing). Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app posts your score at the end of a coast, and your total, to the game's leaderboards, where it appears next to your Game Center nickname. The Friends tab shows scores of your Game Center friends. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can sign out of it in your device's Settings. The game is fully playable without it.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app receives only confirmation that the purchase exists. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
