PRIVACY · EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 20, 2026

Lantern Coast collects no data.

No server of its own, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. (This replaces the October 15, 2026 policy; the substance is unchanged.)

On your device

Your best score and stars per coast, and your settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best per coast and your total are posted to its leaderboards under your Game Center name. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy. You can sign out in Settings and keep playing everything.

The full-coast pass

Sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the game only learns that you own the pass. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Children, changes, contact

Nothing is collected from anyone, children included. A changed policy will appear here with a new date. Questions: braznichenko@gmail.com.
